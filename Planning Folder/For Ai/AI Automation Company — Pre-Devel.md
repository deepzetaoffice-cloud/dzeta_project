## 0. Purpose of this document
This document defines the **engineering standard** the new AI Automation company website should inherit from the existing Wasleen Liminal Approvals site — while designing a completely different visual identity and content model.
The existing site is a strong technical base. What it provides is not a design to copy, but a **repeatable, provable methodology** for:
- Entity-first structured data (one connected JSON-LD graph)- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) via `llms.txt` / `llms-full.txt`- Centralized analytics with a single event taxonomy- A strict build sequence with verification gates- A rule-file system that keeps AI-assisted development safe
Everything below is portable. Only the visual layer and the content vocabulary should differ.

## 2. Decisions to Lock BEFORE Any Design Work
These decisions block everything downstream. Do not let design or content generation begin until they are resolved.
| # | Decision | Options | Impact ||---|---|---|---|| D1 | **Domain strategy** | New apex domain vs. `automation.wasleen.com` subdomain vs. fully separate brand | Canonical URLs, schema `@id`, SEO inheritance || D2 | **Entity model** | One legal company with two brands, or two separate companies | NAP consistency, `Organization` entity, Google Business Profile || D3 | **Market / locale** | UAE-only / GCC / Global-English | hreflang, GBP vs. global SEO, currency in schema || D4 | **Business model** | Agency (services) / Productized SaaS / Hybrid | `Service` vs. `SoftwareApplication` schema, pricing pages, URL map || D5 | **Primary conversion** | Booked call / lead form / free audit / self-serve trial | Tracking events, funnel pages, CRO || D6 | **Language scope** | EN only, or EN + AR | Doubles routing, schema, and content effort |
**Recommendation:** If the AI automation brand serves **global / English-first B2B**, launch **EN-only on a distinct domain** and skip the `/ar` layer initially. Port the bilingual architecture later only if GCC demand proves out. This avoids inheriting ~40% of the operational complexity that exists in the current site purely because Dubai market dominance requires bilingual parity.

## 3. Portable Technical Foundation (Non-Negotiable Standards)
- **Next.js 15 App Router only** — Metadata API for all tags; route handlers for `llms.txt` and `sitemap`.- **Design tokens first** — every color, spacing value, and type size maps to a token. One `tailwind.config.ts` equivalent plus CSS variables. Dark-first is acceptable for an AI/tech aesthetic, but must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.- **`next/font` self-hosted** — prefer a **variable font** (e.g. Inter / Geist) to avoid the manual preload-hash maintenance problem documented in [src/app/layout.tsx](../src/app/layout.tsx). One variable font equals one woff2 and a simpler critical path.- **`next/image` everywhere** — explicit `width` / `height`, `loading="lazy"` below the fold.- **No JavaScript-only navigation** — real server-rendered `<a href>` links.- **Semantic landmarks** — `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`.- **Performance budget** — keep the same hard caps:  - Total page weight (HTML + CSS + JS): under 150KB  - Critical-path JS: under 50KB  - Render-blocking resources: zero  - LCP under 1.5s, CLS under 0.05, INP under 100ms- **Motion discipline** — for a tech brand, animation is tempting. Gate all motion behind CSS plus `prefers-reduced-motion`, and never ship a heavy animation library on the critical path.

## 4. Information Architecture & URL Structure
The AI automation niche has a **much wider keyword surface** than Dubai approvals. Model it as hubs to clusters to leaf pages.
```mermaidflowchart TD    H[Homepage /] --> S[Services Hub /services]    H --> SOL[Solutions Hub /solutions]    H --> IND[Industries Hub /industries]    H --> CS[Case Studies /case-studies]    H --> RES[Resources Hub /resources]    H --> PRIC[Pricing /pricing]    H --> AB[About /about-us]    H --> CT[Contact /contact-us]
    S --> S1[/services/ai-chatbots]    S --> S2[/services/workflow-automation]    S --> S3[/services/ai-voice-agents]    S --> S4[/services/crm-automation]    S --> S5[/services/document-processing]
    SOL --> SOL1[/solutions/lead-qualification]    SOL --> SOL2[/solutions/customer-support-automation]    SOL --> SOL3[/solutions/invoice-processing]
    IND --> I1[/industries/real-estate-ai-automation]    IND --> I2[/industries/healthcare-ai-automation]    IND --> I3[/industries/ecommerce-ai-automation]
    RES --> R1[/resources/how-to-automate-lead-followup]    RES --> R2[/resources/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier]    RES --> R3[/resources/ai-agent-roi-calculator]    RES --> BLOG[/blog/slug]```
### Page-type to schema mapping (port and extend)
| Page type | URL pattern | Schema stack ||---|---|---|| Service | `/services/{slug}` | `Service` + `FAQPage` + `HowTo` + `BreadcrumbList` + `WebPage` || Solution (use-case) | `/solutions/{slug}` | `Service` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` || Industry | `/industries/{slug}` | `Service` + `FAQPage` + `ItemList` || Case study | `/case-studies/{slug}` | `Article` or `CaseStudy` + `BreadcrumbList` || Comparison / Q&A | `/resources/{slug}` | `QAPage` or `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` || Blog post | `/blog/{slug}` | `BlogPosting` + `Person` author + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` || Pricing | `/pricing` | `Product` / `Offer` (if productized) + `FAQPage` |
**Key difference from approvals:** add **`/solutions`** (outcome language — "automate lead follow-up") and **`/industries`** (vertical pSEO). Buyers search by *problem and industry*, not by service name. This is the biggest organic opportunity and the current site has no direct equivalent.

### Additions specific to AI automation
- `SoftwareApplication` — for productized tools and agents. Include `applicationCategory: "BusinessApplication"` and `offers` for pricing.- `OfferCatalog` — bridging services. Reuse the pattern already proven in [src/lib/blog-schema.ts](../src/lib/blog-schema.ts).- `Person` author nodes — with real credentials. E-E-A-T is even more decisive in the AI/tech space, which is saturated with low-trust content farms.- **Byte-for-byte NAP / single-`@id` rule** — keep it. It is what allows AI engines to resolve the business as a real entity.

## 6. GEO / AEO / AIO Blueprint
The existing [src/lib/geo.ts](../src/lib/geo.ts) implements doctrine most agencies have not discovered yet. Reuse the **mechanics**, rewrite the **formatters**.
### Port verbatim
- `llms.txt` manifest plus `llms-full.txt` knowledge base as Next.js route handlers  ([src/app/llms.txt/route.ts](../src/app/llms.txt/route.ts), [src/app/llms-full.txt/route.ts](../src/app/llms-full.txt/route.ts))- **Robots AI-bot tiering** ([src/app/robots.ts](../src/app/robots.ts)) — live-search bots (`PerplexityBot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `Claude-SearchBot`) get full HTML; training scrapers (`GPTBot`, `CCBot`, etc.) locked to `llms.txt` only.- **Direct-answer blocks** — 2 to 3 self-contained sentences as Section 1 of every page.- **Stats strips** — hard numbers. AI engines quote numbers more than any other content type.- **HTML tables** for pricing, process, and requirements.- **Entity resolution** and **marketing-fluff stripping** — the `resolveEntities()` and `stripMarketingFluff()` concepts.
### Rewrite for AI automation (new entity vocabulary)
- Replace authority-name resolution with **technology and vendor entity resolution** — n8n, Make, Zapier, OpenAI, Anthropic, LangChain, HubSpot, Salesforce. AI engines need explicit product names, never "the platform".- Add a **glossary / definitions cluster** ("What is an AI agent?", "What is RAG?"). Definitional content is disproportionately cited by AI Overviews.- Add **comparison pages** (`n8n vs Make vs Zapier`, `AI agent vs RPA`). Comparison content is heavily favored by ChatGPT and Perplexity.- Every ~150 to 200 words should contain one quotable fact (benchmark, percentage, time saved, cost).
**Structural trap to avoid:** do not strip all marketing. AI automation buyers expect ROI claims — just make them **specific and sourced** (e.g. "reduced response time from 4.2 hours to 11 minutes for a 12-person team") rather than "industry-leading".

## 7. Tracking & Analytics Architecture
Port the **architecture**, replace the **events**. The wrapper pattern in [src/lib/analytics.ts](../src/lib/analytics.ts) plus the Pixel bridge in [src/lib/meta-pixel.ts](../src/lib/meta-pixel.ts) is the right model.
```mermaidflowchart TD    UI[Components] -->|trackEvent| T[lib/analytics.ts]    T -->|sendGTMEvent| DL[window.dataLayer]    DL --> GTM[GTM Container]    GTM --> GA4[GA4]    GTM --> GADS[Google Ads]    GTM --> LI[LinkedIn Insight Tag]    T -->|metaContact metaLead| FBQ[Meta Pixel]    FBQ --> CAPI[Server-side Conversions API]    LI --> LICAPI[LinkedIn Conversions API]    FORM[Lead Forms] -->|PII only| CAPI    FORM --> CRM[CRM HubSpot or Pipedrive]    CRM --> OFFLINE[Offline Conversion Import to Ads]```
### Non-negotiables to port
- Single GTM container. **Never** hand-paste gtag snippets.- Early `dataLayer` initialization — [src/app/layout.tsx](../src/app/layout.tsx) already does this and documents exactly why. Keep it.- Never call vendor APIs directly from components — always through wrappers.- **Defer third-party scripts past LCP** — reuse the idle-scheduling `AnalyticsLoader` pattern.- **PII never through GTM** — only through server-side APIs and the CRM (see the rule in [src/lib/analytics.ts](../src/lib/analytics.ts)).
### New elements required for a B2B AI company
| Requirement | Why ||---|---|| LinkedIn Insight Tag plus Conversions API | The #1 B2B paid channel; not present in the current site || Server-side GTM (Vercel plus GTM server container) | Ad-blocker resilience, first-party cookies, better match quality || Consent Mode v2 | GDPR / UK GDPR if serving EU or UK; UAE PDPL if local || Click-ID capture (`gclid`, `fbclid`, `li_fat_id`, `msclkid`) into hidden form fields, then CRM | Closes the loop for offline conversion import || UTM discipline plus first-touch / last-touch storage | Attributes long B2B sales cycles || Lead scoring event | Produces MQL / SQL signals for sales |
### Draft event taxonomy (replaces the approvals event set)
| Event | Trigger | Category ||---|---|---|| `cta_click` | Any primary CTA | engagement || `view_service` | Service or solution page view | content || `pricing_view` | Pricing page view | intent || `case_study_view` | Case study 75% scroll | intent || `calculator_complete` | ROI calculator used | micro-conversion || `generate_lead` | Form submit success | lead || `book_call_click` | Calendly / Cal.com click | lead || `contact_click` | WhatsApp / phone / email | contact || `newsletter_signup` | Footer or modal | lead || `resource_download` | Gated asset | lead |

- **Primary CTA** repeated throughout: "Book a free automation audit" — higher intent than "Contact us".- **Interactive ROI calculator** — doubles as a GEO asset (it generates numbers) and a lead magnet.- **Multi-step quote / qualifier wizard** — port the pattern from the existing free-quote build (progressive disclosure, validation, success state, step-by-step tracking).- **Floating WhatsApp only if UAE-relevant** — otherwise use a floating "Book a Call" widget.- **Trust strip** — client logos (with permission), tool certifications and partner badges (n8n / Make partner, OpenAI / Anthropic ecosystem), security posture (SOC 2 / ISO / GDPR).- **Case studies with hard metrics** — the single strongest E-E-A-T and GEO asset for this niche.

## 13. Pitfalls to Explicitly Avoid
- **Do not copy the approvals content model** — the "documents / timeline / cost" tables do not fit software. Use "capabilities / integrations / ROI / time-to-value".- **Do not inherit the `/ar` complexity** unless GCC is confirmed as primary.- **Do not skip LinkedIn tracking** — it is the biggest channel gap versus the current site.- **Do not let motion and animation wreck Core Web Vitals** — a tech brand invites heavy hero animations.- **Do not launch productized pricing without `Product` / `Offer` schema** if you sell self-serve.- **Do not reuse the current `Organization` `@id` or NAP** if this is a different legal entity — that would corrupt entity resolution for both brands.
