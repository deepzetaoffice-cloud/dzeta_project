## 1. AI Automation

### 1A. AI Agents & Assistants

#### 1A.1 WhatsApp AI Agent 🔥
An AI assistant on the WhatsApp Business API that replies instantly in Arabic and English, 24/7.
- Answers questions from the business's own information
- Qualifies leads and books appointments
- Sends quotes, catalogues, location and payment links
- Hands over to a human with the full conversation
- Broadcasts and follow-up campaigns (opt-in only)
- **Best for:** clinics, real estate, retail, restaurants, service companies

#### 1A.2 AI Voice Receptionist 🔥
An AI that answers phone calls, in Arabic and English, like a trained receptionist.
- Answers every call, including after hours and during peak times
- Books, reschedules and cancels appointments in the calendar
- Answers common questions and takes messages
- Transfers urgent calls to staff
- Sends a call summary and transcript to the CRM
- **Best for:** clinics, dentists, real estate, technical services, car services

#### 1A.3 AI Outbound Calling Agent ⭐
- Calls new leads within minutes of an enquiry
- Appointment reminders and confirmations

#### 1A.4 Website AI Chat Agent ⭐

### 1B. Sales & Lead Automation

#### 1B.1 Speed-to-Lead System 🔥
Every new lead gets a reply within 60 seconds.
- Captures leads from ads, website, WhatsApp, portals (Bayut, Property Finder, Dubizzle) and calls
- Instant WhatsApp, SMS or email reply
- Optional instant AI call-back
- Routes the lead to the right salesperson
- Alerts the manager if nobody responds

#### 1B.2 AI Lead Qualification & Scoring ⭐
- Asks qualifying questions automatically (budget, timeline, need)
- Scores each lead from its answers and behaviour
- Sends hot leads to sales and puts cold leads into nurture
- Sends conversion data back to Google Ads and Meta for better targeting

#### 1B.3 Automated Quotation & Quote Tracking System 🔥
From enquiry to signed quote with little manual work, and full visibility of every quote.
- **Quote creation:** quotes generated from a form, WhatsApp chat, email or CRM deal, using price lists and rules
- **Branded documents:** PDF or online quotes in English and Arabic with logo, terms and VAT
- **Approvals:** manager approval for discounts or amounts above a limit
- **Sending:** by email and WhatsApp with a secure link
- **View tracking:** see when the client opens the quote, how many times, and for how long
- **Status pipeline:** Draft → Sent → Viewed → Accepted / Rejected / Expired
- **Automatic follow-ups:** reminders on a schedule if the quote is not accepted
- **E-signature & acceptance:** client accepts or signs online, with optional deposit payment
- **Conversion:** accepted quote becomes a job, project, order or invoice automatically
- **Dashboard:** quote value, win rate, average time to close, lost reasons, per salesperson
- **Best for:** technical services, fit-out, construction, maintenance, B2B suppliers, agencies

#### 1B.4 Proposal Automation ⭐
- Generates tailored proposals from templates and CRM data
- AI writes the client-specific sections for staff to review
- Tracks opens and sends follow-ups

#### 1B.5 Sales Follow-Up & Nurture Sequences ⭐
- Multi-step WhatsApp, email and SMS sequences
- Different paths based on how the lead responds
- Stops automatically when the lead replies or books

#### 1B.6 CRM Setup & Automation ⭐
- CRM setup (HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Odoo)
- Pipelines, stages, tasks and reminders
- Automatic data entry from forms, calls, email and WhatsApp
- Duplicate cleanup and data enrichment
- Sales dashboards

#### 1B.7 AI Sales Prospecting & Outreach ➕

- Follows UAE and destination-country outreach and data rules
