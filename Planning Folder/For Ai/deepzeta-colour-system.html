1 · Core brand coloursFrom the logo

The six colours that make up the mark and wordmark. Navy and white carry the page; the four blues and violet carry the brand.

2 · Support tonesSecondary shades

Lighter and more readable versions of the core hues, for glows, links, button fills and violet text.

3 · Neutral scaleNavy to white

Every surface, border and text colour on the site comes from this one ramp, tinted toward the brand blue.

4 · Status coloursFeedback and alerts

Kept separate from the brand accents so success, warnings and errors are never confused with a call to action.

5 · GradientsTen signature blends

G1 and G2 come straight from the logo ribbon and the "zeta" wordmark. Copy the CSS under each one.

6 · The four pixelsOne colour per service

The four squares in the logo can stand for the four services, and work as icons and category colours across the site.

7 · ReadabilityWhat can carry text

WCAG contrast measured live. 4.5 or more passes for body text; 3 to 4.5 is only for large headings of 24px or more.
