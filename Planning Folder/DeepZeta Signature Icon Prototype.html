01 · The story

Messages and calls come in, and a booking lands in the calendar

The whole story plays once in 1.55 seconds when the icon scrolls into view. Only transform and opacity are animated, with no JavaScript animation library.

  1. 0.00–0.70sThe calendar settles. The brand-colour depth layer slides out beneath the glass calendar (from C).
  2. 0.15–0.60sA message arrives. The chat bubble pops in and its voice waveform bounces: WhatsApp and phone in one shape.
  3. 0.25–0.65sThe week fills in. Open time slots appear one by one.
  4. 0.55–1.39sThe booking lands. The logo's four pixels, in their exact colours, sizes and arrangement, fly in at the ribbon's 35° angle and settle on the booked slot, breaking out of the frame just as the logo pixels break away from the Z (from B).
  5. 0.95–1.55sDone. One light sweep crosses the calendar (from C) and the main pixel glows.
