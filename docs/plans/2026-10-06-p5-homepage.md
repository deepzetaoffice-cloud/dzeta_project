## The JavaScript budget (07 §2, 13 §7, decision 0021)

- **First load today:** 11,077 B of our own JS (cap 11,264 B) — **187 B headroom**. The plan's rule: **no new first-load modules**. All P5 interactivity (FAQ chips/copy-link/deep links, story replay, the LCP stamp, demo panels) is delivered by lazy modules that load on first pointer/focus/in-view, through one shared loader (`lazy.ts`, target ≤ 0.2 KB of first-load wiring) and the already-lazy click path (`clicks.ts`).
- **Each lazy module is measured** per 13 §7's method (minified, sibling imports external, gzip 6) against its cap in the effect register, and the measurement is reported.
- **`OWN_JS_HOME` is raised by the measured first-load delta only** (expected ≈ 0–200 B; the loader wiring). If the measured delta doesn't fit under 11,264 B, work stops and the owner decides (a cap change is a new decision, 0021 precedent) — nothing is silently slimmed or absorbed.

## Amendment — S7's lab gates (owner, 2026-10-06)

S7's local lhci run on the real Home (2026-10-06, 15 runs) stopped on two hard limits:
- **LCP:** median 2,707 ms on the European profile (allowance 2,550, C61) and 2,706 ms on the UAE campaign profile (2,500). Performance stayed 0.96–0.97, TBT 12–24 ms (Europe) and 58–60 ms (UAE), CLS 0.
- **Page weight:** Home's first-party HTML + CSS + JS was 200,042 B against 190,868 B (+9,174 B), and the review page 192,937 B against its 192,000 B (+937 B). JavaScript was within budget (148,405 B against Home's 150,932 B).

The owner's decisions:
1. **LCP:** "Accept as an exception within decision 0020", then **2,750 ms with the plan amendment.** C63 gives Home a 2,750 ms lab allowance on both profiles. The 2.5 s hard limit (07 §1) stays for real visitors, checked in field data after launch.
2. **Page weight:** "**Slim first, then decide**".
   - **The slimming pass:** it moved Home's own section CSS into a route stylesheet (`home.css`, imported by the Home route), which took about 1.6 KB gzipped off every other page. Home itself can't get lighter without cutting content. The extra bytes are React sending the page twice (markup and page data), and gzip's 32 KB window can't compress copies that far apart. Production (Vercel) serves Brotli, which does: Home's first-load bodies are 194,921 B gzipped and 174,217 B in Brotli, about 179 KB with headers, under the hard limit.
