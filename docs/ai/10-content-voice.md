# 10 · Content & Brand Voice

> **Applies to:** all website copy (English now; Arabic rules in [11](11-i18n-rtl-readiness.md) §3) · **Precedence:** below 00 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-26

---

## 1. Voice

- **Confident, concrete, useful.** Write like a senior consultant talking to a busy business owner.
- Lead with **outcomes** ("every lead gets a reply within 60 seconds"), not technology ("AI-powered synergy").
- Plain language: short sentences (≤ 20 words on average), active voice, second person ("you"), present tense.
- Speak to UAE/GCC realities (WhatsApp-first customers, bilingual audiences, local platforms), without clichés.
- **Show, then tell:** link claims to live demos or real results on the site.

## 2. Naming

- Service names come from `Planning Folder/For Ai/DeepZeta Services Catalogue.md` and are used **exactly**.
- Brand: **deepzeta** (lowercase in running text as in the logo), "deepzeta · AI Digital Solutions" in formal contexts.
- Always write **AI** (never "Ai" or "ai").

## 3. Claims

1. **No invented facts** (see [02](02-anti-hallucination-and-edit-safety.md) §1). Stats, results, clients, testimonials, logos and partner badges need owner confirmation and, for results, a source.
2. **No guarantees** of rankings, revenue or results. Use measured results from real projects.
3. **No unproven superlatives** ("best", "world-class", "#1", "leading") unless backed by a named, verifiable source.
4. Partner/certification badges (Google Partner, Meta Partner, n8n, etc.) only if the owner confirms the status is real and current.
5. Legal/regulatory statements (UAE PDPL, e-invoicing dates, health-data rules) cite the official source and are re-checked before publishing.

## 4. Banned words and patterns

leverage (as a verb), utilize, synergy, cutting-edge, revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, robust, unlock, empower, elevate, supercharge, "in today's fast-paced world", "look no further", "we are passionate about", lorem ipsum, "Welcome to our website", filler intros, and generic stock claims that could describe any agency.

## 5. Page copy structure (default)

1. H1: plain statement of what the page offers.
2. Direct answer (2–3 sentences, quotable alone).
3. Outcomes / who it's for.
4. How it works (steps with real timeframes if confirmed).
5. Proof (demo, case study or measured result).
6. FAQ (real buyer questions).
7. One clear call to action: **Book a free AI audit**.

## 6. Accessibility of content

Descriptive link text, real alt text (decorative images use `alt=""`), no information conveyed by colour alone, headings that describe their section.
