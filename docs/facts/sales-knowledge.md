**Rules for agents**

- Only entries marked **APPROVED** reach the agent: its knowledge (`npm run agent:knowledge` writes `agent-knowledge.md`, which is generated and never edited by hand) or its rules (`clients.config.rules`). A **DRAFT** entry is never used.
- Timelines, response times and other commitments here are the owner's own. Nothing is added from memory or from other agencies' norms (00 N3).
- Prices stay out until the owner confirms them in `company-facts.md` §3; the agent says every project is quoted after the free session.
- Service names are the catalogue's, exactly. The offer keeps its official name, "Free AI Automation Audit"; in conversation the agent may call it "a free 30-minute session with one of our experts" (owner, 2026-10-12).
